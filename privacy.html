Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

HustleCenter operates the website hustlecenter.co.za. We are a local services marketplace based in Polokwane, South Africa. Our Information Officer can be reached at help@hustlecenter.co.za.

2. What we collect

We collect only the information needed to provide our service. This includes:

  • Entrepreneurs: Business name, contact name, phone number, email address, service information (category, description, pricing, service area), and any profile photo or logo you choose to upload.
  • Customers: Name, phone number, email address (if provided), service requested, and any message you include with your request.
  • Website visitors: Basic technical information such as IP address and browser type, used for security and analytics purposes only.

We do not collect ID documents, selfies, proof of address, or bank details.

3. Why we collect it

We use your information to:

  • Display approved entrepreneur listings on the marketplace
  • Facilitate connections between customers and service providers
  • Communicate with you about your listing, request, or account
  • Improve and protect our platform
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How we protect it

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information. This includes secure hosting, access controls, and encryption where appropriate. Only authorised HustleCenter personnel can access private information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as it is needed to provide our service or as required by law. If you request deletion, we will remove your information within a reasonable timeframe, subject to any legal obligations we may have.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information:

  • When you have explicitly chosen to make it visible on your public profile
  • With service providers who help us operate the platform (e.g., hosting, email delivery)
  • When required by law or to protect our rights

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, email us at help@hustlecenter.co.za. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

We may use essential cookies to ensure the website functions properly. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect how the website works.

9. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or concerns, contact us at help@hustlecenter.co.za.
