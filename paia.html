PAIA Manual

Prepared in accordance with the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000

1. Contact details

Information Officer: The HustleCenter Information Officer
Email: help@hustlecenter.co.za
Website: hustlecenter.co.za
Location: Polokwane, Limpopo, South Africa

2. Purpose of this manual

This manual explains how to request information from HustleCenter in terms of the Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA) and the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). It describes the records we hold, how to access them, and the process for making a request.

3. Information we hold

HustleCenter holds the following categories of records:

  • Entrepreneur data: Business name, contact name, phone number, email address, service details, and uploaded photos.
  • Customer data: Name, phone number, email address (if provided), service requests, and messages.
  • Connection records: Logs of requests and connections made through the platform.
  • Business records: Operational documents, correspondence, and administrative records.

4. How to request information

To request access to information, follow these steps:

  • Complete Form C from the SA Information Regulator website (inforegulator.org.za).
  • Email the completed form to help@hustlecenter.co.za.
  • Include sufficient detail to identify the records you are requesting.
  • We will respond within 30 days as required by PAIA.

5. Fees

There is no fee for requesting your own personal information. For other requests, the fees prescribed under PAIA may apply. We will inform you of any applicable fees before processing your request.

6. Grounds for refusal

We may refuse a request if:

  • The information is protected by legal privilege
  • Disclosure would violate someone else's privacy
  • The request is manifestly frivolous or vexatious
  • Disclosure is prohibited by law

7. POPIA compliance

We are committed to protecting personal information in accordance with POPIA. Our full Privacy Policy is available at hustlecenter.co.za/privacy.

8. Availability

This manual is available at hustlecenter.co.za/paia and from the South African Information Regulator.
