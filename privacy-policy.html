HustleCenter Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 27 April 2026

1. WHO WE ARE

HustleCenter operates www.hustlecenter.co.za. Contact: help@hustlecenter.co.za.

2. WHAT WE COLLECT

  • Entrepreneurs: Name, ID Number, ID Photo, Selfie, Proof of Address, Bank Details, Service Info.
  • Customers: Name, Phone Number, Email, Service Requested.
  • Website: IP Address, Device Info (for security).

3. WHY WE COLLECT IT

To verify identities, prevent fraud, connect customers to entrepreneurs, and comply with POPIA.

4. HOW WE PROTECT IT

ID documents are deleted after 90 days. Request logs are kept for 2 years for dispute resolution. We use OTP to verify customer phone numbers.

5. YOUR RIGHTS

You may request access to, correction, or deletion of your personal information. Email help@hustlecenter.co.za. We respond within 30 days.

6. DISPUTE & SAPS COOPERATION

If a crime is reported, HustleCenter will cooperate with SAPS and provide logged information as required by law (with a valid CAS number or court order).
