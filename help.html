Help & FAQ

Answers to common questions about using HustleCenter.

For Customers

How do I find a service provider?

Visit our Browse Services page. You can search by keyword or filter by category to find the right professional for your needs.

Is it free to use HustleCenter as a customer?

Yes. Browsing services and connecting with providers is completely free for customers.

How do I contact a provider?

When you find a provider you're interested in, you can submit a request through their profile page. You'll be asked to provide your name, phone number, and what service you need.

Does HustleCenter guarantee the quality of work?

No. HustleCenter facilitates introductions between customers and service providers. We review listings before publication, but we do not guarantee the quality, safety, or outcome of any service. Always confirm details directly with the provider before work begins.

What should I do if I have a problem with a provider?

If you experience an issue, please contact us at help@hustlecenter.co.za. While we are not a party to your agreement with the provider, we take reports seriously and may remove providers who violate our terms.

For Entrepreneurs

How do I list my service?

Visit our List Your Service page and complete the form. It's free during our launch period.

How long does it take for my listing to appear?

Every listing is reviewed by our team before publication. We typically review submissions within 1–2 business days. You'll be notified once your listing is approved.

Can I edit my listing after it's published?

Yes. Contact us at help@hustlecenter.co.za with the changes you'd like to make, and we'll update your listing.

What if my listing is rejected?

If your listing is rejected, we'll let you know why. Common reasons include incomplete information, inappropriate content, or listings that don't match our categories. You can correct the issue and resubmit.

How do customers contact me?

Customers can submit a request through your profile page. You'll receive their details including name, phone number, and what service they need.

General Questions

Where is HustleCenter available?

HustleCenter is currently available in Polokwane, Limpopo, South Africa. We plan to expand to other cities over time.

How can I contact HustleCenter?

You can reach us at help@hustlecenter.co.za or visit our Contact page for more options.

Is my personal information safe?

We take privacy seriously. Read our Privacy Policy to understand what information we collect and how we protect it.
