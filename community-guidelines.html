Community Guidelines

Our expectations for everyone using HustleCenter.

1. Be respectful

Treat others the way you'd want to be treated. Communicate politely, avoid harassment, and respect people's time. Abusive language, threats, or discriminatory behaviour will not be tolerated.

2. Be honest

Provide accurate information in your listings, requests, and communications. Don't misrepresent your services, prices, qualifications, or availability. Misleading others damages trust in the entire community.

3. Keep it professional

HustleCenter is a marketplace for legitimate services. Don't submit listings for illegal activities, prohibited items, or services that violate South African law. Don't use the platform to spam or solicit unrelated business.

4. Protect privacy

Don't publicly share other people's personal information without their permission. Respect the contact preferences that providers have chosen. If a provider has chosen to be contacted by email, don't insist on WhatsApp.

5. Report problems

If you see a listing that violates these guidelines, or if you've had a negative experience with a provider or customer, let us know at help@hustlecenter.co.za. We take reports seriously.

6. Consequences

Violating these guidelines may result in:

  • Your listing being removed
  • Your account being suspended
  • Removal from the platform where appropriate.
  • Referral to law enforcement if required

7. We're all building this together

HustleCenter is new and growing. The way you use the platform today shapes what it becomes tomorrow. Help us build a marketplace that Polokwane can be proud of.
