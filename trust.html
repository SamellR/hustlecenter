Trust & Safety

Every connection starts with trust.

HustleCenter is built to help people discover local services and connect with providers. We take reasonable steps to foster a trustworthy environment, but it's important to understand what we do — and what we don't do.

Listings are reviewed

Before a service appears on HustleCenter, our team reviews the listing for completeness, relevance and compliance with applicable HustleCenter requirements. This helps us maintain a useful and trustworthy marketplace.

A listing passing review does not automatically mean that HustleCenter has independently verified every claim made by the provider or confirmed their identity, qualifications, experience, business registration or background.

HustleCenter currently uses a Reviewed Listing model, not a general identity-verification or background-check system.
A published listing means it has passed HustleCenter's listing review process. It does not mean that HustleCenter guarantees the provider or their services.

What listing review means

Review may consider appropriate factors such as:

  • Whether required listing information has been provided.
  • Whether the listing fits an available marketplace category.
  • Whether the description appears relevant to the advertised service.
  • Whether the listing complies with HustleCenter rules.
  • Whether submitted images/content appear appropriate for publication.
  • Whether obvious misleading or prohibited information is present.

What review does not mean

A reviewed listing does not necessarily mean HustleCenter has:

  • Confirmed every statement made by the provider.
  • Verified professional qualifications.
  • Conducted a criminal background check.
  • Confirmed business registration.
  • Confirmed ownership of a business.
  • Guaranteed the provider's work.
  • Guaranteed availability.
  • Guaranteed pricing.
  • Guaranteed customer satisfaction.
  • Guaranteed that a transaction will be successful.

False or misleading listings can be removed

If a listing contains inaccurate, misleading, inappropriate or prohibited information, HustleCenter may review the matter and take appropriate action, including removing or restricting the listing.

Actions may include:

  • Requesting clarification.
  • Correcting information.
  • Removing content.
  • Rejecting a listing.
  • Restricting a listing.
  • Suspending an account.
  • Removing access to the platform.

We take reports seriously

Users can report problems involving listings, providers, customers or website functionality. Reports may be reviewed according to the circumstances and available information.

Account restrictions

If a user repeatedly violates HustleCenter rules or engages in harmful or prohibited behaviour, HustleCenter may take appropriate platform action.

Possible actions include:

  • Warning.
  • Content removal.
  • Listing rejection.
  • Listing removal.
  • Account restriction.
  • Account suspension.
  • Account termination where appropriate.

Customers should confirm details directly

Before agreeing to a service, customers should discuss important details directly with the provider.

  • What service will be provided.
  • Price or quotation.
  • Timing.
  • Location.
  • Materials or additional costs.
  • Payment arrangements.
  • Any other important expectations.

HustleCenter facilitates connections but does not control the agreement between the customer and provider.

Basic safety guidance

Before agreeing to a deal

  • Ask questions if something is unclear.
  • Confirm the provider's details.
  • Discuss the price and scope of work.
  • Be cautious about unusual urgency or pressure.
  • Do not share unnecessary sensitive information.

Payments

Users should understand the payment arrangements before paying. HustleCenter does not provide refunds, payment protection or transaction guarantees.

Meeting someone

Where appropriate, consider reasonable personal-safety precautions when meeting someone you first encountered online.

  • Meet in an appropriate place.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going.
  • Consider having someone accompany you where appropriate.
  • Do not assume that being listed on HustleCenter guarantees personal safety.

Entrepreneurs should communicate honestly

Providers using HustleCenter are expected to:

  • Describe their services accurately.
  • Provide truthful information.
  • Communicate professionally.
  • Avoid misleading claims.
  • Honour commitments they make to customers.
  • Follow HustleCenter's Terms and Community Guidelines.

Misleading information can damage trust between entrepreneurs and customers and may result in platform action.

HustleCenter facilitates connections

HustleCenter provides a platform where customers and entrepreneurs can discover each other and communicate.

HustleCenter does not automatically control:

  • The customer's decision.
  • The provider's work.
  • The agreed price.
  • The provider's availability.
  • The quality of completed work.
  • The customer's satisfaction.
  • The outcome of a private transaction.

HustleCenter's exact legal role and limitations are governed by the Terms of Service and Disclaimer.

Cooperation with lawful requests

Where legally required or appropriate, HustleCenter may cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities. Actual handling must follow applicable law and HustleCenter's Privacy Policy.

Trust does not mean guaranteed outcomes

HustleCenter takes reasonable steps to support a trustworthy marketplace, but no online platform can eliminate every risk. Users should make their own decisions, verify important details where appropriate, and use reasonable safety precautions.

Reviewed Listing vs Identity Verification

Reviewed Listing means the listing has gone through HustleCenter's applicable marketplace review before publication.

Identity Verification means a person's identity has been checked through a defined verification process.

These are not automatically the same thing. Do not call an ordinary reviewed listing "identity verified."
